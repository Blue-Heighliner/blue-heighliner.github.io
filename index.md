# 👋 Welcome
 
We're a group of volunteers who build free, open source tools and libraries, no strings attached.
 
Everything here is released under the **MIT License**. Use it, fork it, embed it in a commercial product, rip it apart and repurpose it. You don't need our permission, and you never will.
 
## 🎯 What we're about
 
- **Free, forever.** No paid tiers, no "open core," no bait-and-switch relicensing down the road.
- **No restrictions.** MIT means MIT, do what you want with the code.
- **Built by volunteers, for whoever finds it useful.** We work on this because we like solving problems, not because anyone's paying us to.
