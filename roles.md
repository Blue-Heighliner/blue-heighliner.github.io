# Roles
 
The Heighliner organization defines three roles across its repositories. Each role builds on the permissions of the one before it.
 
## Managers
 
- Oversee the Heighliner ecosystem as a whole
- Have control over tags in all repositories
- Manage final releases through release tags

## Maintainers
 
- Manage merges into the development branches of specific repositories
- Review and approve contributions from others
- Merge in work they have done themselves
